# Position and facing on story maps

`location` marks the photographed place. An optional `viewpoint` marks a researched,
approximate modern starting point: latitude, longitude, a short footpath/landmark
label, a source URL, and optionally a clockwise bearing from north. Do not copy
place coordinates into viewpoint coordinates or infer a walking route from an
arrow. Verify the archive view and modern pedestrian geography separately.

Keep north up. The camera symbol and wedge show facing direction, not optical
field of view, a measured camera pose, or a promise that the photographs align.
A visitor's selected point is temporary, stays in their browser, and points toward
the place marker. It must be labelled as their choice, never as the historic
photographer's position. Unresearched places retain a place marker only until a
visitor picks a spot. GPS is requested only explicitly; accuracy is approximate.

## Ferry Building correction

Auckland Libraries [1-W1360](https://kura.aucklandlibraries.govt.nz/digital/collection/photos/id/98506)
identifies an eastward view from Albert Street corner. Previous Queen Street and
Queens Wharf wording was wrong. The proposed modern starting point
(-36.84286, 174.76594) is on [mapped sidewalk 951667282](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/951667282),
not a surveyed historical position. Public access/obstructions still depend on
conditions on the day.

The subject pin is the approximate centre of
[Ferry Building footprint 23904427](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/23904427),
(-36.84300, 174.76688). The bearing from the suggested point is about 100°.
The old place pin was near the roadway and must not be used as a standing point.

For future images, consult the archive record and inspect the image before adding
viewpoints; record the modern map evidence here. Keep the directions useful even
if map tiles or location permission are unavailable. Map changes must preserve
one-finger page scrolling and existing comparison geometry.
